Six seeds, six needs.

Every seed here is kept for something Lebanon will need. Open a seed to see its passport.

Tal Amara · 900 m

From the slope to the bank

How a seed enters the collection.

It starts with listening. Botanists record local farming names, the weather and seasonal habits before a seed goes into cold care.

01

Collect

In the field, at peak ripeness.

02

Document

Name, place, altitude, soil, story.

03

Clean

By hand, seed by seed.

04

Dry

Slowly, so the seed can rest.

05

Cold care

At −20 °C, in the dark.

06

Test

Checked regularly, to be sure it can still wake.
