Why it matters

We never see where the things we love begin.

Bread, wedding flowers and Christmas lights all start below the ground, in a seed someone kept.

Tal Amara · 900 m

01The root is the need

What we need from a plant was there from the start.

  • PineIts roots are the Christmas lights.
  • MagnoliaIts roots hold the wedding rings.
  • BambooIts roots weave the basket.

02Lebanon works the same way

Every loaf of saj starts with emmer wheat.

Every man’ouche starts with a za’atar seed smaller than a grain of salt. Every pot of mujaddara starts with a lentil someone kept.

Every seed is an old soul waiting to live.

03Time in the valley

We measure this work in generations.

A seed placed in cold care today may wake sixty years from now, in the hands of a farmer not yet born.

When modern hybrids falter under shifting seasons, the old seeds hold answers. They have lived through droughts that history books forgot.

04When the land calls

They are not the past. They are what we will need next.

The seed goes back to research nurseries, its lines are grown out, and it returns to Lebanese farmers. This is why the bank exists: to provide what Lebanon will need, when the time comes.
