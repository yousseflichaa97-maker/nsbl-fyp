Getting here

Through the Chtaura crossroads.

From Beirut. Cross the Dahr el-Baidar pass toward Chtaura, then head east past Zahlé toward Rayak. The station is on the main agricultural access road.

From north or south. Take the central Bekaa road that links Baalbek in the north with Rachaya and the Western Bekaa in the south.

This is a working scientific station. Please confirm your visit in advance, so our staff can arrange laboratory access.

Holding an old variety?

Your baladi seed may hold your children’s harvest.

Farmers, researchers and teachers are all welcome to write to the bank.
